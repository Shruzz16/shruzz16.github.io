Manufactured a **20-tooth Module 0.5 brass involute spur gear** using a **rotary CNC setup**. The project focused on connecting **gear geometry, custom workholding, CNC machine setup, G-code generation, and multi-pass machining** to produce a small functional gear.

---

## Project overview

- Manufactured a small **involute spur gear** from brass
- Used a **Module 0.5 involute gear cutter**
- Prepared an **11 mm brass gear blank**
- Designed a **custom arbor** to hold the cutter rigidly
- Used a **rotary CNC setup** to index the gear tooth-by-tooth
- Generated **Python-based G-code** for controlled machining

---

## Design objectives

- Manufacture a functional 20-tooth brass spur gear
- Understand the relationship between **module, tooth count, pitch diameter, and outside diameter**
- Design a rigid cutter-holding arbor to reduce bending during cutting
- Set up the rotary CNC system for accurate gear indexing
- Use multi-pass machining to reduce cutting load and improve tooth quality

---

## Gear specification

For the selected gear:

- **Gear type** = Involute spur gear
- **Material** = Brass
- **Module** = 0.5
- **Number of teeth** = 20
- **Face width** = 3 mm
- **Pitch diameter** = 10 mm
- **Outside diameter** = 11 mm

Basic gear calculation:

Pitch Diameter = Module × Number of Teeth

Pitch Diameter = 0.5 × 20 = 10 mm
